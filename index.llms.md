# Welcome

I am a research economist from the University of Oregon working in behavioral and experimental economics, game theory, and data science. My research includes out-of-the-lab experiments which reveal longitudinal time preferences and strategic behavior in the presence of Artificial Intelligence. More broadly, I am interested in designing experiments, extracting practical information from the data they generate, and modeling key economic behaviors using a modern microeconomic theory.
